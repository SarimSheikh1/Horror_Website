The Dark Ritual

The ancient text is clear. Five candles. One pentagram.
Light them all and the gateway opens.
Once begun, the ritual cannot be stopped.

📜 The Ancient Instructions

Light the five sacred candles in sequence. As each flame ignites, speak the name aloud. When all five burn together, the pentagram awakens. The boundary between worlds grows thin. What comes through cannot be sent back.

"Ex tenebris lux — ex luce tenebrae"
"From darkness, light — from light, darkness"

The Five Sacred Flames

⛧

First Flame — IGNIS

The Fire of Awakening

The first candle calls to the sleeping entity. It stirs in the place between dreams and death. It becomes aware that a ritual has begun. It turns its attention toward the flame. At this point you may still stop. The cost is small.

Second Flame — TENEBRA

The Fire of Darkness

The shadows in the room deepen. Not metaphorically — the ambient light decreases measurably as the entity absorbs it. It is feeding on the darkness you create. Witnesses have reported hearing their own names whispered from empty corners at this stage.

Third Flame — MORTIS

The Fire of Death

You cannot stop now. The entity will not allow it. The third flame burns with the colour of old blood. The barrier is now thin enough for impressions to pass through — emotions not your own, images behind your eyes, a presence standing just out of sight.

Fourth Flame — ANIMUS

The Fire of Souls

The fourth flame summons the gateway. Something is on the other side. It presses against the barrier the way a face presses against glass. Four candles is enough to see it. Not enough to stop it from seeing you back. From this point it will know your face.

Fifth Flame — LUX

The Fire of No Return

The last candle completes the summoning. The gateway opens fully. The entity crosses the boundary. It is now in the room with you. It has been waiting for this ritual for a very long time. Every version of it that has ever been performed, it remembers. It remembers you, specifically. Because you have done this before. You just don't remember.
