The Horror Gallery

Each piece was found, not created.
Hover at your own risk. Some images bite.

Scanning exhibits for anomalies…

👹

Exhibit 001 — The Devourer

Found in Siberia, 1934. Photographer unknown.

"The subject was photographed over 48 hours. In each subsequent image, the figure is closer to the camera. The final image shows only darkness and two reflective eyes."
👻

Exhibit 002 — The Bride of Morrow House

Morrow House, 1887. Anomaly unexplained.

"The woman in white appears in the background of over 200 photographs taken at Morrow House across six decades. She has never aged. She is always watching the camera."
🪆

Exhibit 003 — The Listening Doll

Romania, 1962. Confiscated by authorities.

"Every owner of this doll reported the same experience: waking at 3 AM to find the doll positioned differently. Four owners are deceased. The doll's expression changes between photographs."
🏚

Exhibit 004 — Gallows Road #7

Photographed at 3:14 AM, 2023.

"The house has been empty since 1993. The light in the upper window has been burning continuously for 31 years. The electricity was disconnected on October 14, 1993."
🕷️

Exhibit 005 — The Nest

Amazonia, 2019. Expedition log Entry 19.

"The nest was the size of a small room. The expedition team photographed it at 14:00. By 14:07 all six members had vacated the area. They declined to explain why. Three have not spoken publicly since."
🪞

Exhibit 006 — The Hargrove Mirror

Retrieved from Gallows Road. Currently sealed.

"Three curators have resigned since the mirror entered the collection. Each reported the same incident: their reflection continuing to move after they had stopped. The mirror is now kept covered at all times."
🌀

Exhibit 007 — The Doorway

Origin unknown. Submitted anonymously.

"The photograph depicts a doorway in an unidentified location. Within the darkness beyond, digital analysis reveals the outlines of approximately 14 distinct humanoid figures. All appear to be facing outward."
🤡

Exhibit 008 — Visitor #47

CCTV still, children's ward, 2018.

"The figure appeared on CCTV footage at 3:14 AM. No visitor signed in. No visitor signed out. Staff on duty that night reported hearing laughter in empty corridors. The footage covers a 6-hour window. The figure appears in 847 individual frames."
🌲

Exhibit 009 — The Last Photo

Found on a recovered camera, Grimvale Forest.

"The camera was found half a mile from the forest edge. The memory card contained 412 photographs taken over 72 hours — far longer than the hiker was reported missing. The final photograph is entirely black except for two points of light. Analysis suggests they are eyes. Very close to the lens."
📼

Exhibit 010 — The Tape

VHS. 1987. Contents classified.

"The tape contains 7 minutes of footage. The first 6 minutes are static. In the final minute, a child's voice counts backwards from 10. At zero, the static clears briefly. No one who has watched the complete tape has agreed to describe what appears."
🕯️

Exhibit 011 — The Vigil

Photograph taken in an empty room.

"The candles were placed in the room and ignited. The photographer left immediately and triggered the shutter remotely. In the photograph, a figure stands among the candles. The candles cast no shadow on it. It casts a shadow on the candles."
🌊

Exhibit 012 — The Deep

Underwater photograph, Pacific, 2021.

"Taken at 4,200 metres depth by an unmanned probe. The object in the frame does not appear in any marine biology database. It appears to be looking at the probe. The second photograph, taken 3 seconds later, shows only empty water. The probe's onboard microphone recorded something that the team described as 'not a sound that water should be able to make.'"
😱
