7 FASL ROMAN URDU + ENGLISH ☠ MAUT KE MANZAR

Fasl Awwal — Chapter One

The House Nobody Spoke Of

Woh Ghar — Jis Ka Koi Zikr Na Karta

Nobody on Gallows Road spoke of the house at number seven. Not the old woman who sold fruit at the corner, not the children who played until dark, not even the stray dogs — they crossed the road to avoid its shadow.

Gallows Road par number saat wale ghar ka koi zikr nahi karta tha. Na woh budhiya jo coron par phal bechti thi, na woh bachche jo andhera hone tak khelate the, yahan tak ke aawara kutte bhi — woh us ghar ki chhaon se bachne ke liye sadak par doosri taraf chale jaate the.

You moved in on a Tuesday. The estate agent smiled too wide. He handed you the keys without looking at the door.

Tum Mangal ke din andar aaye. Agent bahut zyada muskura raha tha. Usne chabi dete waqt darwaaze ki taraf dekha hi nahi.

That night, behind the walls, something breathed.

Us raat, deewaron ke peechay, kuch saansen le raha tha.

Fasl Doum — Chapter Two

The Mirror That Remembered

Woh Aaina — Jo Yaad Rakhta Tha

On the third day you found the mirror in the cellar. Six feet tall. Black wood frame carved with symbols you did not recognise. The glass was perfectly clear — not a trace of dust, as though someone had polished it that morning.

Teesre din tumne ghar ke andar ek aaina paya. Chhe foot uncha. Kaale lakdi ka frame jis par ajeeb nishaanaat khude hue the. Shesha bilkul saaf tha — jaise kisi ne aaj subah saaf kiya ho.

You stood before it and straightened your shirt. Your reflection straightened its shirt three seconds later.

Tum us ke saamne khade hue aur apni qameez seedhi ki. Tumhari parchhain ne apni qameez seedhi ki — teen second baad.

You ran. At 3:14 AM something knocked on your locked bedroom door — from the inside.

Tum bhaage. Raat 3:14 baje, andar se band darwaaze par kisi ne dastak di — kamre ke andar se.

Kya tum darr gaye? Aage parhne se pehle — ek lamhe ke liye aankhein band karo.

🩸

Fasl Soum — Chapter Three

The Children in the Garden

Bagheeche Ke Bachche

On the fifth morning you saw them. Two children beyond the iron fence. A boy and a girl. Woollen coats in the August heat. They were facing your window. Perfectly still.

Paanchwe din subah tum ne unhe dekha. Do bachche — lohe ki jangla ke paar. Ek larka, ek larki. August ki garmi mein uni coaton mein. Tumhare khirki ki taraf munh kiye, bilkul khamosh, bilkul saakoon.

You watched for twenty minutes. When the phone rang and you looked away for one second — they were gone.

Tum ne bees minute dekha. Jab phone bajaa aur ek lamhe ke liye nazar hati — woh gayab the.

You searched online. You found the newspaper photograph from October 1993.

Tum ne internet par dhundha. October 1993 ka akhbaar mila.

The same children. From 31 years ago. Staring from the screen.

Wohi bachche. 31 saal pehle ke. Screen se tumhe ghoor rahe the.

Umar: 8 saal. Aakhri baar 14 October 1993 ko dekha gaya. Kabhi nahi mila.

Fasl Charum — Chapter Four

The Writing Behind the Tiles

Tiles Ke Peechay Likha Hua

A loose tile fell from the bathroom wall. Behind it, scratched into plaster — the same phrase, written over and over until it covered every inch:

Bathroom ki ek tile girti hai. Peechay, plaster par khuncha hua — ek hi jumla, baar baar, jab tak har inch chhup na jaye:

DON'T OPEN THE BASEMENT DOOR

TAH KHANA KA DARWAAZA MAT KHOLNA

That night the basement door opened by itself. Cold air. The smell of earth and copper. From the darkness below, a sound. A voice — in your own voice — said:

Us raat tah khane ka darwaaza khud khul gaya. Thandi hawa. Mitti aur taamba ki boo. Neeche andheron se awaaz aayi. Teri apni awaaz mein:

"You shouldn't have come down here."

"Tujhe yahan nahi aana chahiye tha."

Tah khana khol dete hain? Darwaaza tumhare liye intizaar kar raha hai...

Fasl Panjum — Chapter Five

The Killer in the Dark

Andheron Ka Qatil

His name — as best the records show — was Hargrove. He lived in the house from 1962 to 1993. The neighbours remembered a quiet man. Always polite. Always watching.

Uska naam — jitna records mein hai — Hargrove tha. Woh 1962 se 1993 tak is ghar mein raha. Parosi yaad karte hain ek khamosh aadmi. Hamesha tarteeb. Hamesha dekhta rehta.

When police entered on October 14th, 1993, they found the dinner table set for five. Three plates of cold food. Two chairs overturned. The photographs on the wall — all facing inward.

Jab police 14 October 1993 ko andar aayi, khane ki mez par paanch logon ke liye setting thi. Teeen plate thanda khana. Do kursiyan ulti pari thin. Deewaron ki tasveereen — sab andar ki taraf munh kiye hue.

Hargrove was never found.

Hargrove kabhi nahi mila.

The last entry in his diary read: "They're in the walls now. All of them. Waiting."

Us ki diary ki aakhri satren: "Woh ab deewaron mein hain. Sab. Intizaar kar rahe hain."

Fasl Shashum — Chapter Six

The Last Night

Aakhri Raat

By the seventh day you stopped sleeping. You sat in the hallway with every light burning and watched the shadows. At 3:14 AM the entire house went dark. Not a power cut — the streetlights still burned. Every bulb simply died, simultaneously.

Saatwen din tak neend chhoot gayi. Tum darwaaze par baithe rahe, har bulb jala ke, andheron ko dekhte rahe. Raat 3:14 baje poora ghar andhere mein doob gaya. Bijli nahi gayi — bahar ke batti jal rahi thin. Har bulb ek saath bujh gaya.

Then came the footsteps. Slow. From the basement. Up the stairs. Stopping outside your door.

Phir qadam aye. Aahista. Tah khane se. Seedhiyon par. Tumhare darwaaze ke bahar ruk gaye.

Three knocks. The same three knocks from that first night.

Teen dastak. Wohi teen dastak — pehli raat wali.

You did not open the door. You packed a bag at dawn and walked out and did not look back.

Tum ne darwaaza nahi khola. Subah ek thaila uthaya aur bahar nikal gaye. Peechay nahi dekha.

Fasl Haftum — Final Chapter

It Followed You Home

Woh Tumhare Peeche Aa Gaya

Three weeks later. Your city apartment. You had almost convinced yourself it was over. You slept again. You laughed again.

Teen haftay baad. Tumhara shehar wala flat. Tum ne apne aap ko samjha liya tha — sab khatam ho gaya. Phir se neend aayi. Phir se hansi aayi.

On a Tuesday night — exactly one month after moving in — you woke at 3:14 AM.

Ek Mangal ki raat — theek ek mahine baad — raat 3:14 baje aankhein khulein.

The room was cold. In the corner, where the shadow fell thickest, something was standing.

Kamra thanda tha. Kone mein, jahan andhera ghana tha — kuch khada tha.

It tilted its head. And in the mirror on the wall, your reflection was already sitting up — watching you — before you moved.

Us ne apna sar jhukaya. Aur deewaar ke aaine mein — tumhari parchhain pehle se baitha tha. Tumhein dekh raha tha. Tum hile nahi the abhi.

. . .

Kya tum abhi bhi parh rahe ho?

Peechay dekho. Abhi. Seedha.

Are you still reading? Look behind you. Right now.
