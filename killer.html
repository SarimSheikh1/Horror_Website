Subject Background — Taruf

Who Is Hargrove?

Hargrove moved into 7 Gallows Road in 1962. Neighbours described him as quiet, polite, and deeply unsettling in ways they could never articulate. He worked from home. He never had visitors. Lights in his house burned all night, every night. Hargrove 1962 mein 7 Gallows Road mein aaya. Parosi kehte the — khamosh, shareef, lekin andar se kuch ajeeb. Woh ghar se kaam karta tha. Koi nahi aata tha. Ghar ki roshniyan raat bhar jali rehti thin.

Between 1962 and 1993, five people entered the house. None left. Records show all five victims were found in the basement. Below the floor. Arranged in a circle. 1962 se 1993 ke darmiyan, paanch log ghar mein gaye. Koi nahi nikla. Records kehte hain — sab paanch log tah khane mein mile. Farsh ke neeche. Daaire mein.

On October 14, 1993, police forced entry. Hargrove was gone. His diary's final entry: "I hear them calling. Time to find a new house. A new family." 14 October 1993 ko police ne darwaaza toda. Hargrove ja chuka tha. Diary ki aakhri satar: "Mujhe awaazein aati hain. Naya ghar dhundna hai. Naya khaandaan."

His Method — Uska Tarika

How He Chooses

He does not choose at random. Investigators believe he watches first — sometimes for weeks. He learns routines. Sleep times. Fears. Woh randomly nahi chunti. Investigators ka kehna hai pehle woh dekhta hai — kabhi kabhi haftoon tak. Woh aadat seekhta hai. Neend ka waqt. Darr.

Entry is always at 3:14 AM. Always through a door that was locked. No signs of forced entry have ever been found. Andar aana hamesha raat 3:14 baje. Hamesha kisi band darwaaze se. Kabhi toota hua darwaaza nahi mila.

The last thing victims see: their own reflection moving independently in a nearby mirror. Then the lights go out. Then the knocking begins. Shikaaron ka aakhri manzar: apni parchhain — jo unke bina hil rahi hoti hai. Phir roshni bujhti hai. Phir dastak shuru hoti hai.

Active Warning — Khabardar

If You See Him

Do not run. Running confirms your location. Do not speak. He listens for voices. Do not look in mirrors after midnight. Bhago mat. Bhaagna tumhari jagah bata deta hai. Baat mat karo. Woh awaazein sunta hai. Aadhi raat ke baad aaine mein mat dekho.

If you are reading this at night, alone — he may already be watching. Agar tum ye raat ko akele parh rahe ho — woh shayad pehle se dekh raha hai.

Ek aakhri manzar. Agar dekh sako toh. — One last image. If you can bear it.
