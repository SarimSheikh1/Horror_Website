Walk The Corridor

🚶

Entry — 11:47 PM

The Padlock Is Already Open

The chain on the main gate hangs loose. Someone has been here recently. Your torch beam sweeps across the entrance hall — cracked tile floors, peeling walls, a reception desk with a ledger still open to a page dated October 14, 1962. The last entry reads: "They won't stop screaming. I can't make them stop."

Corridor B — 11:52 PM

The Patient Is Still There

Halfway down Corridor B you find a wheelchair. In it sits a figure — until your torch reaches it and you see it's a bundle of old clothes, arranged to look like a seated person. Deliberately arranged. Someone positioned this recently. You hear a door close somewhere above you.

Ward 13 — 12:01 AM

Thirteen Beds. Fourteen Names.

The ward register is still on the wall. Thirteen patient names. But at the bottom, in different handwriting, a fourteenth name has been added. The ink is fresh. The name is yours.

Sub-Level 3 — 12:19 AM

You Are Not Alone Down Here

The staircase goes down further than it should. At the bottom, a long corridor with a single light at the end — fluorescent, buzzing. Seven doors, all closed. As you approach the light, you realise the buzzing has rhythm. It's breathing. The door at the end has a keypad. Someone has recently typed a code — the buttons are worn. 1, 4, 6, 2. The year the asylum closed.
